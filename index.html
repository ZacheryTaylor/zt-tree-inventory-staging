Accuracy & limits

Accurate tree data. Approximate locations.

Accurate — measured in the field:

  • Tag number on a physical tag on the tree
  • Species as identified and recorded
  • DBH as measured at 4.5 ft
  • Field notes and photos

Approximate — for illustration only:

  • Phone GPS points, typically ~10–30 ft (GPS.gov); canopy makes it worse
  • Sketch positions, for field reference only
  • Not a survey. The tag on the tree is the reference, and your surveyor locates the tags for the sealed drawing.

Service area

The Florida Panhandle.

The same full package in every area. Protected/heritage suggestions use each jurisdiction's published thresholds. Parcel lines from each county's public GIS.

Not offered: surveys or survey-grade locations; engineering or surveying advice; preparing or sealing tree protection or landscape plans (Pensacola requires a Florida-licensed landscape architect, architect, or civil engineer); arborist services, including tree risk, hazard, or condition opinions and F.S. 163.045 danger-tree letters.
