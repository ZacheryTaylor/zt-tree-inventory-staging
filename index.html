Accuracy & limits

Honest about what GPS can do.

Phone GPS is typically good to about 10–30 ft under open sky, per GPS.gov. Tree canopy and buildings make it worse. That's fine for planning and counting. It is not fine for final site plans.

  • Locations approximate, for field reference only.

  • Coordinates checked against NOAA NCAT transformations.
