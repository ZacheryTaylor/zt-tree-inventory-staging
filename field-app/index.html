Install on iPhone

In Safari tap Share › Add to Home Screen. The installed app works offline and keeps its data separate from Safari tabs; export a backup before deleting it.

How to use

  1. The app always opens on the current job; there is nothing to set up. Tap the job name at the top any time to add client, site, parcels or jurisdiction (all optional). Jurisdiction drives the suggested protected/heritage flags.
  2. Capture: stand at the trunk, tap Capture in the bottom bar, wait for the readings to average. Watch the accuracy readout; above the job limit you get a hard warning.
  3. Pick species (search or recent chips), DBH (or combine stems), canopy, condition. Tap Save. The tag # advances automatically. Undo appears for a few seconds after each save/delete.
  4. Trees: tap any tree to edit. When editing you choose whether to keep the saved position or use a new fix.
  5. Check: capture known points (corner pins, monuments) to measure the phone's real error.
  6. Export: report PDF, PNEZD + notes, tree log, photos, backup, parcel sketch. Then Finish job & start new; finished jobs are under More › Past jobs.

Accuracy limits

Averaging helps against random jitter but not against bias from buildings and canopy (multipath). The accuracy number is the phone's own estimate, not a measured error; use the Check tab for real numbers.
